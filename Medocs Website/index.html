FAQ
Common questions
How does Medocs work with my existing EHR system?
Medocs integrates effortlessly with most existing EHR platforms, allowing for seamless note generation and direct transfer of patient data. It functions as an AI overlay. You keep your current system and Medocs handles the documentation layer.
Is Medocs HIPAA-compliant?
Yes, Medocs is fully HIPAA-compliant. All patient information is encrypted at rest and in transit. We support Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) and maintain comprehensive audit logs across the entire platform.
Can I customize the note templates?
Absolutely. Medocs allows you to tailor templates and documentation styles to your specific medical specialty. We support PT, OT, internal medicine, pediatrics, psychiatry, and more, with custom field configuration available.
How does Medocs handle multiple users in a practice?
Medocs supports multi-user environments with role-based access control. Administrators can configure permissions for providers, billing staff, front desk, and facility managers. Multi-site deployments are supported.
What languages does the AI Scribe support?
Medocs supports over 40 languages across 130 countries. You can record conversations in any supported language and receive structured clinical notes in English, with accuracy preserved across all language combinations.
Is there a mobile app available?
Yes. Medocs is available on both Google Play and the Apple App Store. The mobile app offers the full AI Scribe experience optimized for on-the-go clinical documentation.
